`main.ts` copies `entry.png` (a 1024×1024 front page: 35 dated stories across
7 regions — COP31 in overtime, three missiles downed over Riyadh, the M6.7
Peru quake) into the output directory. Every story happened, or was first
reported, on Saturday 21 November 2026; disputed claims are attributed, not
stated as fact. All graphics are original flat-vector drawings; no photos.
